## Suggested editorial checklist

1. Verify historical content against heritage authorities and a second relevant source where possible.
2. Record the source link, revision date and approving editor in a future editorial audit log.
3. Separate verified facts from storytelling interpretation, traditional claims and artistic embellishment.
4. Verify media license, author credit and model/photo consent before publication.
5. Review English/Hindi translation accuracy with fluent local reviewers.
6. Avoid offensive or inaccurate claims regarding cultural or religious history; use respectful labels.
